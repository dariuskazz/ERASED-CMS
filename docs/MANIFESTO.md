# ERASED CMS Manifesto

ERASED CMS exists to give people control over their websites without forcing them into one hosting provider, one workflow, one editor, one database forever, or one commercial ecosystem.

## Freedom

The website belongs to its owner. Content, configuration, themes, language packs, and packages must remain exportable and portable.

## Privacy

Privacy is the default, not a premium feature. ERASED CMS must not require telemetry, cloud accounts, or external services to operate.

## Self-hosting

ERASED CMS should run on ordinary PHP hosting, VPS environments, and container platforms without unnecessary operational complexity.

## Modularity

The core provides stable services. Packages provide features. The core should become smaller and more stable as the ecosystem grows.

## Choice

Users should be able to choose their website profile, theme, editor, search provider, commerce provider, authentication method, AI provider, and other capabilities.

## Longevity

A site installed today should remain upgradeable for years without requiring a fresh installation or destructive migration.

## Transparency

Updates, package permissions, migrations, AI suggestions, backups, and destructive actions must be visible and understandable.

## Optional AI

AI may assist, suggest, search, translate, diagnose, or draft. It must never be mandatory, silently change the site, or replace normal workflows. The administrator remains in control.

## Human-friendly and developer-friendly

Basic administration should be understandable without technical expertise. Extension development should be possible without modifying the core.

> **Freedom without complexity.**
